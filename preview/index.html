About

Websites that work as hard as you do

I'm Rakibul Hasan, a web developer and SEO professional. I help businesses get online with websites that are fast, clean and easy for customers to use.

A good-looking site is only half the job — so I also work on SEO, helping those websites show up on Google where customers are actually searching.

My main project is BexoBD, an ecommerce website builder that lets sellers launch their own online store. I also handle SEO work for live business websites, including bexobd.online.

Whether you need a new website, an online store, or more visitors from Google, I keep the process simple: clear communication, honest work, and a site built to grow with your business.
